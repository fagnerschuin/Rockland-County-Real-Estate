What Goes Into a Local Home Valuation

Online estimates are a starting point, but they cannot see your kitchen, your yard or the street you live on. A local valuation can.

We look at recent sales of similar homes nearby, homes currently for sale and homes that did not sell, then compare them to your home's size, layout, updates and condition.

We also factor in the things that move value in Rockland County, like school district, lot size, commute access, whether a basement is finished and how the home compares to others on the block.

You get a clear, realistic range and an explanation of how we got there. There is no obligation to list.
