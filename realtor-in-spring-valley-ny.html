Buying and Selling in Spring Valley

Spring Valley is one of the busiest and most diverse villages in Rockland County. It has a strong mix of two-family houses and older homes, which makes it a natural fit for buyers who want help with the mortgage from a rental unit.

A lot of Spring Valley housing was built before the 1960s, and many homes have been split into two units over the years. Some of those conversions are legal and some are not. Before buying, we help you check the certificate of occupancy with the village so you know how many units the home is approved for.

Expect older single-family homes, two-family houses, small multi-family buildings and a number of condos and co-ops. Many homes have been updated in stages, so we look closely at heating, electrical and roof age.

Parts of Spring Valley fall in the East Ramapo Central School District, and some streets near the village edges follow different lines. We help you confirm the district for each address.
