Buying and Selling in Nyack

Nyack is Rockland's river town. The village sits on the Hudson right by the Governor Mario M. Cuomo Bridge, with a lively Main Street and Broadway full of restaurants, galleries and shops.

Nyack draws buyers who want character, walkability and water views. Homes with original details and a short walk to Broadway are in steady demand, as are condos close to the river.

Nyack has many Victorians and early 1900s homes, plus village row houses, newer condos and some mid-century homes on the hills above the river.

Older homes can bring stone foundations, knob and tube wiring, slate roofs and lead paint. Some streets are within historic districts, which can affect exterior changes. We help you plan inspections and ask about past work and permits.
