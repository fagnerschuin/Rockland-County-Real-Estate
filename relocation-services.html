Moving to Rockland County

Many people moving to Rockland County are coming from New York City, Westchester, Bergen County or farther away. We help you understand the area before you commit.

We explain how the towns differ: where the train lines run, which areas have quick access to the Thruway or the Governor Mario M. Cuomo Bridge, and how school district lines work, since they do not always match the town name on the mailing address.

If you cannot be here often, we can set up video tours, share neighborhood notes and line up a focused tour day when you visit.

We also help you time a sale in one place with a purchase here, and connect you with local attorneys, inspectors and lenders.
