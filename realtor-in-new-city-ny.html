Buying and Selling in New City

New City is the county seat of Rockland and a favorite for families who need more room. Many neighborhoods were built in the 1960s through the 1980s with bigger lots, larger colonials and plenty of split levels and high ranches.

New City attracts move-up buyers from smaller homes elsewhere in the county, as well as buyers from Westchester and Bergen County who want more space. Homes that have been updated inside while keeping a good yard tend to stand out.

Expect four-bedroom colonials, raised ranches, split levels and some custom homes on wooded or hilly lots. Many have original kitchens and baths that buyers plan to update.

On hillier lots, drainage, retaining walls and driveway slope are worth a look. Most of New City feeds into the Clarkstown Central School District, but we confirm the district and elementary school for each address.
