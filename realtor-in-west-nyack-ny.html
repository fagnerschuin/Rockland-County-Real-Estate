Buying and Selling in West Nyack

West Nyack puts you close to almost everything: the Palisades Center, Route 59, Route 303 and quick access to the Thruway and the bridge. It is also a place where a little homework before you buy goes a long way.

Parts of West Nyack sit near the Hackensack River and the Demarest Kill, and some low-lying areas have a history of flooding in big storms. Many homes are nowhere near a flood zone, but it is worth checking every address before you fall in love.

West Nyack has a mix of 1950s capes and ranches, 1960s and 1970s split levels, and some newer townhome communities. Lot sizes vary a lot from street to street.

We help buyers look up FEMA flood maps, ask about flood insurance needs early and review any water history in the seller's disclosure. West Nyack addresses can fall in either the Clarkstown or Nyack school districts, so we confirm that too.
