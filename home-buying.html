Buying a Home in Rockland County

Good homes in Rockland County can move quickly. We help you get ready ahead of time, so when the right one comes up, you can act with confidence.

We start by learning what matters to you: commute, school district, yard size, layout, budget comfort zone and how long you plan to stay. Then we point you to the towns and streets that fit.

When you find a home you like, we look closely at what the listing does not say, like the age of the roof and heating system, whether the house is on public sewer, and whether it sits in a mapped flood zone. Then we help you write an offer that is competitive and sensible.

After your offer is accepted, we keep the inspection, attorney review, appraisal and mortgage commitment on track, and we walk you through the final walk-through and closing.
