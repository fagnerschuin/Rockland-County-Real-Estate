Buying and Selling in Valley Cottage

Valley Cottage is a great fit for people who want the outdoors close to home. Rockland Lake State Park and Hook Mountain are right next door, and the Governor Mario M. Cuomo Bridge is only a short drive away.

Valley Cottage has a good mix of single-family homes and townhome or condo communities, which gives buyers more options at different stages of life. Townhomes here are popular with first-time buyers and people downsizing from larger houses.

Single-family homes are mostly 1950s through 1980s ranches, capes and split levels. There are also several townhome and condo communities with shared amenities.

For townhomes and condos, we help you review HOA rules, reserve funds and what monthly fees cover. Many Valley Cottage addresses are in the Nyack school district, but we confirm it for each home.
