Your First Home Purchase in Rockland County

Your first purchase comes with a lot of new words and steps. We slow it down and explain each one, so you feel confident instead of rushed.

We start with the basics: how pre-approval works, what goes into your monthly cost beyond the mortgage, including Rockland County property taxes, and what to expect from the timeline in New York.

We can point you to local lenders who can tell you whether first-time buyer options, such as programs through the State of New York Mortgage Agency, might fit your situation.

From there we help you compare homes, condos and townhouses across Rockland, write your first offer and understand the inspection report. We stay with you through closing day and the handoff of the keys.
