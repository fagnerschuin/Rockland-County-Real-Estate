About Rockland County Real Estate

We are a local real estate team based in Nanuet, helping people buy and sell homes across Rockland County with clear advice and steady support.

Our Story

We see real estate as a people business first. Behind every listing is a family deciding where to live next, and behind every offer is someone hoping they are making the right call. Our job is to give them honest information and a calm, organized process, so the decision feels like theirs and not like something they were pushed into.

Nanuet is our home base, and it shapes how we work. From here we are a short drive to almost every corner of Rockland County, from the river villages in Nyack to the commuter streets in Suffern and the quiet roads of Chestnut Ridge. We spend our days in these neighborhoods, so we know how a home on one block can differ from a home two streets over.

We would rather tell you something you do not want to hear today than have you surprised at the closing table. That means realistic pricing, honest feedback on homes and clear explanations of each step in a New York sale.

How We Work

Honesty first

We give you straight answers about price, condition and timing, even when the news is not what you hoped.

Local knowledge that matters

School district lines, flood zones, commute times and housing age all affect value. We bring that knowledge to every conversation.

Clear communication

You will always know what is happening next, who is responsible for it and when to expect an update.

Your goals lead

We build the plan around your timeline, budget and comfort level, not around a quick sale.
