Selling Your Home in Rockland County

Selling well comes down to three things: the right price, a home that shows well and a steady process after the offer. We handle all three with you.

We begin with a walk-through of your home and an honest conversation about what buyers in your town will notice first. Often small fixes, decluttering and fresh paint matter more than big projects.

We price your home using recent sales of similar homes nearby, then market it with quality photos, a clear description and showings that are easy for buyers to book.

Once offers come in, we help you compare more than the number: financing strength, contingencies and timing all matter. Then we work with your attorney to keep the contract, inspection and closing on schedule.
