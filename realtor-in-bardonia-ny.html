Buying and Selling in Bardonia

Bardonia is a small, quiet hamlet tucked between Nanuet and New City. It does not get as much attention as its neighbors, which is part of its appeal for people who want calm streets with quick access to everything else.

Bardonia is popular with longtime Rockland residents who want to downsize without leaving the area, and with younger families looking for a starter home in the Clarkstown schools. Ranch homes, in particular, get a lot of interest from buyers who want one-level living.

Most homes are ranches, split levels and modest colonials from the 1950s through the 1970s, many on well-kept lots. A good number have had only one or two owners.

Homes with long-time owners can have older boilers, original windows or an underground oil tank from years ago. We help buyers ask the right questions, and we help sellers get ahead of these items before listing.
