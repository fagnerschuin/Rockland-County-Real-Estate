Meet the Team

Our team is built around the moments that matter most in a home sale or purchase, so you always have someone focused on your next step.

The People You Will Work With

Buyer's Agent

Helps you narrow down towns and streets, sets up showings and spots details a listing might leave out. When you find the right home, this role guides your offer and negotiation and keeps your purchase on track through closing.

Listing Agent

Walks through your home, prices it against real local sales and builds a marketing plan that brings in serious buyers. This role handles showings, offers and negotiations so you can focus on your move.

Transaction Coordinator

Keeps the paperwork and deadlines moving once you are under contract. This role stays in touch with attorneys, lenders, inspectors and appraisers so nothing slips through the cracks before closing day.

How We Work Together

Every client starts with a conversation about goals, timing and budget. From there we build a simple plan and share it with you, including what we will do, what we need from you and what happens next. You will hear from us at each step, not only when there is a problem.

We work closely with the attorneys, lenders and inspectors on your deal, so information flows to you quickly and clearly. If something changes, we tell you right away and explain your options in plain language.
