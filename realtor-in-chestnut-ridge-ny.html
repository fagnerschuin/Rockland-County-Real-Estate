Buying and Selling in Chestnut Ridge

Chestnut Ridge sits right on the New Jersey line, next to Montvale, Park Ridge and Woodcliff Lake. That location makes it a smart choice for people who work in Bergen County or use the Garden State Parkway, but still want New York property and a quieter setting.

Buyers here are often after a larger lot, more privacy and a more suburban feel than they would find closer to Route 59. Many come from New Jersey looking for more space.

Expect split levels, colonials and ranches from the 1950s through the 1980s on larger lots, along with some newer construction.

On larger, wooded lots we pay close attention to tree health, drainage and whether the home is on public sewer or a septic system. School district lines vary here as well, so we confirm them for each address.
